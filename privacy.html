Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Simulation Studio ("the app") is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner with the owner's own TikTok account.

Data the app handles

  • Access tokens from TikTok Login Kit. They are stored only on the owner's PC in the Windows Credential Manager and are used solely to upload the owner's own videos.
  • Basic account info (scope user.info.basic), used only to show which account is connected.
  • Upload status (publish ID and status) in a local database on the owner's PC to prevent duplicate uploads.

What the app does not do

The app has no server. It does not collect, sell or share personal data, does not access other users' data and uses no analytics or tracking.

Deletion

Stored tokens can be deleted at any time on the PC; access can also be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Apps and services.
