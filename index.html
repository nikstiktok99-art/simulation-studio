Simulation Studio

Simulation Studio is a personal Windows desktop tool that creates short physics simulation videos (colored balls, races, multipliers) in 9:16 format.

TikTok integration

The tool uploads finished videos from the owner's own PC to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. It uses:

  • Login Kit (Desktop) – the owner signs in with TikTok once in the browser and authorizes the app.
  • Content Posting API, scope video.upload – a video the owner has approved is sent to the TikTok inbox as a draft. The owner then adds the caption and publishes it in the TikTok app.
  • Scope user.info.basic – used only to show which account is connected.

The app does not post publicly on its own, does not read other users' data and is not offered to other users.
